🚌 VISD Bus Transportation

School buses meet morning commuter ferries at the north-end Vashon dock. K-5 students have an official chaperone on the 8:05 AM (or 8:15 AM / 9:30 AM) and 4:40 PM ferries.

🏅 Sports & Activity Late Bus

Students staying after school for sports or clubs ride the late activity bus to the Vashon dock, taking the 4:40 PM or 5:40 PM ferry back to Fauntleroy.

📞 District Transportation

Questions about missing a connection? Call VISD Transportation: (206) 463-4281 or check with the ferry chaperone.
